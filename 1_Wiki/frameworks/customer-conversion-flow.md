# Customer Conversion Flow

## Source

- [[0913-last-week-review]] (0_Inbox/weekly-review/0913-last-week-review.md)

## Definition

The repeatable conversation flow from inquiry to closed order, built from the patterns in customer calls.

## Steps

1. **Ask budget and use case first** — never recommend parts before knowing both.
2. **Send at least two configs** (e.g. balanced vs GPU-heavy) — the comparison sells itself.
3. **Explain the why, not just the parts** — reference the budget-allocation rule; this is where trust is built.
4. **Follow up once** — most orders close after a day of thinking, not in the first chat.
5. **After-sales promise** — software issues are the majority; covering them lowers the "risk of custom".

## When to Use

Every inquiry. It converts the knowledge in the wiki (concepts and comparisons) into a sales conversation.
