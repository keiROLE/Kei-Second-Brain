# Prebuilt vs Custom PC

## Source

- [[2026-09-10]] (0_Inbox/diary/2026-09-10.md)

## Comparison Matrix

| Dimension | Prebuilt | Custom (DIY) |
|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Convenience | plug and play | assembly needed |
| Price for same specs | higher (assembly + brand) | lower (part margins) |
| Part balance | often lopsided (strong GPU, weak PSU) | controllable, balanced |
| Upgradeability | limited by case/board | easy |
| Support | brand warranty | seller support (the studio) |

## Conclusion

Custom wins for price and balance above ~5000 budget; prebuilt wins only for buyers who will never open the case. A seller's job is to make the "custom" choice feel safe — which is what the conversion flow does.
