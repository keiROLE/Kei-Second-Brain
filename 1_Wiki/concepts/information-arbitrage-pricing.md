# Information Arbitrage Pricing

## Source

- [[2026-09-11-doubao-budget-allocation]] (0_Inbox/chat-distill/2026-09-11-doubao-budget-allocation.md)

## Definition

Pricing that captures value from the customer's knowledge gap rather than from the product margin. In DIY PCs, the components are public and prices are transparent — the value a seller adds is **deciding which parts to combine**, and that decision is what the customer pays for.

## Core Insight

The margin is not in the parts; it is in the configuration expertise. This is why explaining *why* a config is chosen (in video and in chat) is the actual sales act — it converts a price comparison into a trusted decision.
