## Background

Alex wanted to know what to verify before buying a higher-refresh-rate monitor.

## Conclusion

Check the resolution and frame rate the game reaches on the intended graphics card. Compare that frame rate with the monitor's refresh rate when estimating what the display can show.

## Excerpt

> Alex: What should I check before paying for a faster screen?
>
> Agent: Check the frame rate the game reaches at the resolution you plan to use. Then compare it with the monitor's refresh rate.
